Our Story — Critical Reflection

Hello Username

As students of Polytechnic we started out with nothing more than a dream and a love for coffee. Long lectures, late-night study groups, group projects coffee became more than a drink for us. It became part of our daily routine, something that kept us going. One day, we were sitting together and talking about how difficult it was to find good coffee at an inexpensive price, and an idea came to life: why not start our own business that sold high-quality coffee beans made for students, by students?

At first, the idea felt too big. We were still studying, with no real business experience and limited money. But we believed in what we wanted to create a small business that sold only the best natural coffee beans at student-friendly prices. We wanted our brand to stand for quality, honesty, and being the best and most well known beans in the country.

When we began, we faced many challenges. One of the biggest was time management. Being full-time students meant our days were already packed, and running a business on top of that was tough. We had to learn how to balance lectures, assignments, and meetings while keeping our business running. Through proper planning and teamwork, we managed to stay on track. Each of us supported the other during busy times and learned that true teamwork is helping one another without question.

Another major challenge we faced was our inexperience with running a business. We had a lot of passion, but we simply didn’t know enough to leverage it. We primarily started out not knowing where to start, how to price our beans, how to share or display our product, and even how to spend our meager budget. Of course we had some learning mistakes along the way, every single one of those being a lesson. We read articles, watched tutorials, and sought advice from professors or small business owners. We began to learn what it meant to plan, budget, and share products.

We also struggled with finding reliable suppliers. We wanted our beans to be the best all-natural, rich, and full of flavor, but many suppliers either charged too much or didn’t meet our quality standards. We spent weeks researching and testing samples until we finally found trustworthy sources that delivered the quality we were proud to sell. That experience taught us the value of patience, communication, and negotiation.

As we progressed with our project, we felt a desire to take our idea further by developing a website that would allow customers to read about our story, see our products, and place orders. None of us were web developers or designers but cybersecurity students, so together we played with the website's design and created a simple, clean, and user-friendly website. Our efforts helped to contribute to our development of new technical skills and helped us understand how to operate a business more generally online.

Through this journey, we learned more than just how to sell coffee beans we learned how to work as a real team. Each of us took part in every task. We didn’t divide responsibilities strictly because we wanted everyone to gain experience in all areas. Whether it was marketing, finance, customer service, or web design, we all contributed equally. . In this way, each member came away with something new and both the leadership and communication skills were enhanced.

We discovered that leadership isn’t about giving orders it’s about guiding, supporting, and inspiring others to do their best. And whenever one of us felt stuck or tired, the rest egged them on. Collaborating proved to us that a good team can do great work with little.

From a technical perspective, we learned a lot about marketing and how to present a product, how to manage the finances, how to sell on the web. We also developed our problem-solving, time management, and planning skills. Most importantly, we discovered the virtue of persistence that excellent things are worth the time, work, and endurance.

Summary of Our Work
We’re a business committed to selling delicious, student-budget friendly, premium, all-natural packaged coffee beans. We’re about specialty coffee that students can sip at home or on campus without bleeding their budget to death.

Together, we worked on every stage of this venture from finding the manufacturers and designing the labels to promoting our products online and shipping orders. We believe that working as a group on every assignment enabled us to complete our work more quickly and become better in everything.

Our experience taught us about reality of the academic and corporate worlds. It emphasized leading and organizing and talking about our experience. And our willingness to stroll sideby side gave us the opportunity to grasp and conquer the difficult and accomplish the – one step at a time.

Towards the future, we’re excited to take our work to the next phase. We want to be roasting and serving coffee and selling beans next! After that, we’d love to open up a mini-café on the Polytechnic campus for students to enjoy fresh brewed coffee with a cool laid back vibe to study or relax. And once that first coffee house is open, we can’t wait to scale to other stores and connect with Delivery Apps, such as Talabat and Jahez, so customers can conveniently order the same products at home.

This project began as a minor thought from students, but it turned into something more substantial, a true learning opportunity, and the start of a journey that we are all proud of. It proved to us that if there are enough people and some courage, students too can make a simple idea fulfilled by teamwork, passion and commitment.

"Good coffee, good code, good times"

- Beans World Team
